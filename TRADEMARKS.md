# Trademark policy

The software and content licenses do not grant rights in the **Filiolae** name, project logos, or
Filiolae certification identifiers. MelaBuilt AI administers these project identifiers to prevent
confusion about source, endorsement, and security status.

## Uses that do not require separate permission

You may:

- truthfully state that unmodified software is Filiolae and identify its exact release or commit;
- state that a project is “based on Filiolae,” “compatible with Filiolae,” or a “fork of Filiolae” when
  the wording is accurate and not more prominent than your own project name;
- link to the project and reproduce the word mark in factual commentary, research, or comparison; and
- retain legally required copyright, attribution, and license notices.

## Uses requiring written permission

Do not without written permission:

- name a modified distribution, product, service, organization, or domain **Filiolae** or a confusingly
  similar variation;
- use a Filiolae logo as your product or organization identity;
- imply sponsorship, endorsement, official status, security assurance, or certification; or
- use `Filiolae Certified`, `Filiolae Verified Integration`, or another reserved certification label.

Forks should choose a distinct name and state the Filiolae version or commit from which they derive.
This policy does not restrict lawful nominative, descriptive, comparative, or other fair use.

No certification program is active for v0.1.0. See [`CERTIFICATION.md`](CERTIFICATION.md). Questions or
permission requests may be opened as a repository issue without including confidential information.
