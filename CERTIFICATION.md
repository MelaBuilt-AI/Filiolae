# Certification policy

**Status: no Filiolae certification program is active for v0.1.0.**

A source checkout, passing test run, adoption-registry entry, contribution, commercial license, or use
of the protocol does not make an organization, model, deployment, or derivative “Filiolae Certified.”
The terms `Filiolae Certified` and `Filiolae Verified Integration` are reserved under the project
trademark policy.

A future certification program, if launched, must publish before accepting applications:

1. exact eligible versions and immutable test materials;
2. evaluator independence, credential/host custody, and conflict-of-interest rules;
3. required positive and fail-closed negative cases;
4. evidence retention, expiry, change-invalidation, appeal, and revocation rules;
5. fees and any waivers;
6. the exact public registry fields and assurance limitations; and
7. a prohibition on upgrading bounded results into production-security or general-quality claims.

Certification must expire and require revalidation after material changes to runtime, adapter, model,
topology, credentials, evaluator, witness, or policy. The clean-room protocol in
[`docs/independent-reproduction-protocol.md`](docs/independent-reproduction-protocol.md) is design input,
not an active certification service.
