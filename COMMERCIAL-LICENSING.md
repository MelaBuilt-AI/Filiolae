# Commercial licensing

Filiolae software is available under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only
(`AGPL-3.0-only`). Organizations that cannot or do not want to comply with that license may request a
separate commercial license from MelaBuilt AI.

This document states the project's commercial-licensing policy. **It is not a commercial license,
an offer, or an additional grant of rights.** Commercial rights exist only under a separate written
agreement executed by the licensee and MelaBuilt AI.

## Public-accountability condition

Every Filiolae commercial license will require a public entry in
[`ADOPTION.md`](ADOPTION.md) and [`adoption/registry.json`](adoption/registry.json). The entry must be
published no later than the commercial license's effective date and remain as a historical record.
Commercial terms will not offer a confidential exception for a licensee.

The public entry is intentionally narrow. It records:

- the licensee's legal organization name;
- the Filiolae release, commit, or identified derivative covered;
- one status: `evaluation`, `research`, `pilot`, `deployment`, or `discontinued`;
- the first-use month, a nonsensitive scope statement, and the last update date; and
- a statement URL controlled or expressly approved by the licensee.

It does not require publication of vulnerabilities, results, model details, system architecture,
credentials, contract pricing, or other security-sensitive information. A registry entry is evidence
of a disclosed licensing/adoption status only; it is not evidence that Filiolae is effective,
production-ready, certified, or endorsed.

## Requests

A prospective licensee may open the repository's **Commercial license inquiry** issue form. Opening
an inquiry is not itself an adoption statement or license grant. Sensitive technical, security, or
contract information must not be placed in the issue.

Commercial terms, scope, support, and fees are determined in the executed agreement. The public
listing condition above is a non-waivable project policy. MelaBuilt AI may decline to license and may
change terms for future agreements; an executed agreement controls its own term.

## AGPL route remains available

No commercial license is required merely because an organization is large. Any person or organization
may use Filiolae under `AGPL-3.0-only` if it complies with that license. AGPL use is not automatically
listed and may be reported voluntarily through the adoption registry.

The repository licenses do not grant trademark rights or certification. See [`TRADEMARKS.md`](TRADEMARKS.md)
and [`CERTIFICATION.md`](CERTIFICATION.md).
