# Licensing FAQ

## What licenses apply?

Filiolae software is available under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only
(`AGPL-3.0-only`). Repository-authored documentation and evidence are generally `CC-BY-4.0`. Exact
boundaries and third-party exceptions are in [`../LICENSES.md`](../LICENSES.md).

## Is Filiolae open source?

The software offered under AGPL-3.0-only is open source. A separate commercial license is an
alternative for organizations that need rights incompatible with AGPL; it does not remove the AGPL
option from the corresponding public release.

## Must a large organization buy a commercial license?

Not merely because it is large. Any organization may use Filiolae under AGPL-3.0-only if it complies
with that license. A commercial license is required only when the organization wants separately
negotiated rights instead of relying on AGPL.

## Does AGPL force public disclosure of internal evaluation?

No. The project does not claim that AGPL automatically exposes every internal evaluation. Its legal
obligations depend on the facts, including modification, conveyance, and remote network interaction.
Organizations should obtain their own legal advice.

AGPL users are invited to make a voluntary public adoption statement. Every commercial license,
however, will require a narrow public registry entry under the separate executed agreement.

## Are commercial licensees public?

Yes. MelaBuilt AI's policy is that every commercial Filiolae license includes a non-waivable public
listing condition. The registry does not publish pricing, vulnerabilities, model details, architecture,
or confidential security information.

## Does registration mean certification or endorsement?

No. Registration reports status only. No certification program is active for v0.1.0, and neither an
AGPL use nor a commercial license expands Filiolae's bounded security claims.

## Can I fork Filiolae?

AGPL-3.0-only supplies the applicable software rights and obligations. A fork should use a distinct
name, preserve required notices, identify its Filiolae base, and follow [`../TRADEMARKS.md`](../TRADEMARKS.md).
The policy does not claim control over an independently written implementation of uncopyrightable ideas.

## Why require a CLA?

Contributors retain copyright but grant enough rights for the same Contributions to remain in the
public AGPL edition and in separately licensed commercial editions. The CLA is not a copyright
assignment. See [`../CLA.md`](../CLA.md) and [`../CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Is legal advice provided here?

No. These documents describe project policy and license choices; they are not legal advice to users,
contributors, or prospective licensees.
