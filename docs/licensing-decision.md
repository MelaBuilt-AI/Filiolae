# Licensing decision — AGPL plus commercial accountability

Status: **selected for the private v0.1.0 candidate; no public grant or commercial agreement issued**

Date: 2026-08-17

## Decision

Filiolae software will be offered under `AGPL-3.0-only`, with separately executed commercial licensing
available from MelaBuilt AI. Repository-authored documentation, policy, adoption-registry content, and
evidence remain generally `CC-BY-4.0`.

Every commercial license must create a narrow public adoption-registry entry no later than its effective
date. The project will not offer a confidential-licensee exception. AGPL users are explicitly invited
to register evaluation and adoption voluntarily.

## Why this balance

Apache-2.0 would maximize permission but allow proprietary incorporation without reciprocal source
obligations or a licensing relationship. A novel entity-size/evaluation-disclosure license would more
directly target transparency, but at pre-alpha maturity its unfamiliarity could prevent the external
review and contribution needed to earn adoption.

AGPL plus commercial licensing is recognizable, keeps a complete public software route, supports small
developers without fees, and creates an accountable contract path for organizations needing alternative
rights. It preserves the possibility of stronger policy in materially improved future versions without
misrepresenting the rights attached to an already released version.

## Exact limitations

- AGPL does not require every organization to announce internal evaluation. Any organization, regardless
  of size, may rely on AGPL if it complies.
- A commercial license purchase becomes public because its separate agreement requires registration,
  not because payment is inherently public.
- The policy covers Filiolae code and copyright derivatives within applicable law; it does not claim
  control over independent implementations of ideas.
- A registry entry is a status disclosure, not proof of effectiveness, independent reproduction,
  production fitness, certification, or endorsement.
- No certification program is active for v0.1.0.
- Rights granted for a public release cannot simply be withdrawn. Later licensing changes do not erase
  compliant rights to earlier versions.

## Contribution and rights continuity

Contributors retain copyright. The narrow CLA grants the Project Steward the non-exclusive rights
needed to keep Contributions in the public AGPL edition and license them under separate commercial
terms. External software Contributions must not be merged without recorded acceptance. Qualified
review of the CLA and acceptance mechanism is required before the first external software merge;
qualified review of rights, agreement terms, fees, and the public-listing condition is required before
the first commercial license is executed.

## Review triggers

Revisit—but do not retroactively rewrite—this decision after independent reproduction, multiple outside
exercise reports, a real external pilot, high-priority recovery hardening, or an enterprise licensing
inquiry. Review evidence and actual adoption friction before considering a custom accountability license
for a future materially distinct version.
